# MIRRORKEEPER

A mirror-copy card duel. You face the MIRRORKEEPER in a hall of mirrors — it
copies the type of the last card you played, telegraphed a full turn ahead on
a giant mirror card in the centre of the table. Vary your types to earn
shards: 3 shards SHATTER the mirror for +8 damage and make the rival skip its
next turn. Or feed the mirror a weak copy on purpose. First to 0 Life loses.

- 30 Life each side, draw 2 per turn, Ember resource.
- 5 card types told apart by colour + shape: Strike (red sword), Guard (blue
  shield), Mend (green leaf), Draw (gold quill), Trick (purple star).
- 30-card deck, hand of 6, keyboard + mouse play.

Built for the make.fun competition "Make the best card game"
(best-card-game-20261122-1826).
